A baby doesn't fit in a two-seater.
> I watched it drive off. Its new owner, tentative. They didn’t know it like I did.
>
> For months something was missing every time I walked down the road. It had sat there nine years. My brain still expected it.
>
> Over the years the metronomic yearning slowed. But it still turned up.
>
> So I made it again. Bright green, cut into four pieces and set apart. Then I sold it again.
>
> Making it got it out of my head. Selling it got it out of the house.
