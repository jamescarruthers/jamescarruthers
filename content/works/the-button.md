This piece represents a moment in time, an event that I only realised the significance after a passing.

> The orange button on the telephone did nothing. I pressed it whilst talking. A stim to soothe my social discomfort.
>
> My parents had me ring my Uncle. Was a 25-year-old classic a good idea for a first car. He asked if I thought it was. I said yes. He said yes.
>
> A Triumph. I came to know every part of it, and the people who knew the rest. It took me across Europe and beyond.
>
> The telephone is long gone, so is the button.
